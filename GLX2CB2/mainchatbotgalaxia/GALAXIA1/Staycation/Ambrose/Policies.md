# Ambrose Villa Policies

- **Pet Policy:** Pets ARE allowed at Ambrose Villas (Extra charge: ₹600 per pet/night).
- **Max Stay Limit:** Maximum stay duration is 3 consecutive nights.
- **Booking Rule:** Official website booking only.
- **Security Deposit:** Refundable deposit required at check-in.

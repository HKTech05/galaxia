# BAMBOOSA (Large Group Private Pool Villa @ Ambrose)

*BAMBOOSA* is a bamboo theme large-capacity private pool villa at Ambrose Villas, Karjat.

## Villa Inclusions & Specs
- Spacious bamboo villa layout with private swimming pool and garden seating.
- 1 SMART TV, 2 ACs, 2 Washrooms, 1 Private swimming pool, 1 Garden sitting area.
- Check-in: 02:00 PM | Check-out: 10:00 AM

## Occupancy Limits
- Max Capacity: 12 Persons Total Limit.
- **Max Adult Limit**: **STRICT MAX 10 ADULTS** (Up to 10 Adults + 2 Kids).
- Kids age range: 5 to 12 years (₹1,000/night). Under 5: Free.

## Tariff Schedule (Excl. 5% GST)
- **MON to THURS**: ₹10,500 for 4 Persons (with meals)
- **Fridays & Sundays**: ₹11,500 for 4 Persons (with meals)
- **Saturdays**: ₹13,000 for 4 Persons (with meals)
- Extra Adult: ₹2,000 per night (meals included).
- Meals Included: Lunch, Dinner, Breakfast (strictly Vegetarian ONLY).
- Security Deposit: ₹3,000 refundable deposit.
- Unit Count: **1 Unit Only**.

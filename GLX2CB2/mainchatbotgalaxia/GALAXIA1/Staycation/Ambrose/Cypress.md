# CYPRESS (Machan Theme Villa @ Ambrose)

*CYPRESS* is a machan/treehouse aesthetic private pool villa at Ambrose Villas, Karjat.

## Villa Inclusions & Specs
- 1 Bedroom with sofa cum bed
- 1 Bedroom with king size bed
- 1 SMART TV, 2 ACs, 2 Washrooms, 1 Private swimming pool, 1 Garden sitting area.
- Check-in: 02:00 PM | Check-out: 10:00 AM

## Occupancy Limits
- Max Capacity: 4 Persons Total Limit.
- **Max Adult Limit**: **STRICT MAX 3 ADULTS** (Up to 3 Adults + 1 Kid).
- Kids age range: 5 to 12 years (₹1,000/night). Under 5: Free.

## Tariff Schedule (Excl. 5% GST)
- **MON to THURS**: ₹5,500 for 2 Persons (with meals)
- **Fri / Sat / Sun**: ₹6,500 for 2 Persons (with meals)
- Extra Adult: ₹2,000 per night (meals included).
- Meals Included: Lunch, Dinner, Breakfast (strictly Vegetarian ONLY).
- Security Deposit: ₹3,000 refundable deposit.
- Unit Count: **1 Unit Only**.

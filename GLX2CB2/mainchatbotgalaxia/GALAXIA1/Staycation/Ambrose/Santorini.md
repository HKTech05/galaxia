# SANTORINI (Greek-Inspired Theme Villa @ Ambrose)

*SANTORINI* is a white-and-blue Greek-inspired theme private pool villa at Ambrose Villas, Karjat.

## Villa Inclusions & Specs
- 1 Bedroom with sofa cum bed
- 1 Bedroom with king size bed
- 1 SMART TV, 2 ACs, 2 Washrooms, 1 Private swimming pool, 1 Garden sitting area.
- Check-in: 02:00 PM | Check-out: 10:00 AM

## Occupancy Limits
- Max Capacity: 8 Persons Total Limit.
- **Max Adult Limit**: **STRICT MAX 6 ADULTS** (Up to 6 Adults + 2 Kids).
- Kids age range: 5 to 12 years (₹1,000/night). Under 5: Free.

## Tariff Schedule (Excl. 5% GST)
- **Mon to Thu**: ₹5,500 for 2 Persons (with meals) | ₹9,500 for 4 Persons (with meals)
- **Fri & Sun**: ₹6,500 for 2 Persons (with meals) | ₹10,500 for 4 Persons (with meals)
- **Saturday**: ₹8,500 for 2 Persons (with meals) | ₹12,000 for 4 Persons (with meals)
- Extra Adult: ₹2,000 per night (meals included).
- Meals Included: Lunch, Dinner, Breakfast (strictly Vegetarian ONLY).
- Security Deposit: ₹3,000 refundable deposit.
- Unit Count: **1 Unit Only**.

# La Paraiso Food Policy

- **Included:** Meals are NOT included in the base tariff.
- **Restaurant Access:** Restaurant is located 10 steps away from villa.
- **Inside Villa:** Vegetarian food is allowed inside the villa.
- **Non-Veg Policy:** Non-vegetarian food must strictly be consumed at the restaurant 10 steps away (NOT inside the villa).

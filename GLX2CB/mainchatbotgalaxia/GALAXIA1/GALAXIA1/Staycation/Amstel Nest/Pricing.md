# Amstel Nest Pricing & Tariff Directory

Amstel Nest features 14 Standard Cottages and 1 Family Cottage with private indoor pools. All meals (Lunch, Dinner, Breakfast - Veg Only) are included in the tariff.

## Rates Schedule (Excl. 5% GST)

- *Standard Cottage* (2 Persons + meals): Mon-Thu *₹4,950*, Fri/Sun *₹5,950*, Saturday *₹6,950*
- *Family Cottage* (4 Persons + meals): Mon-Thu *₹9,000*, Fri/Sun *₹10,000*, Saturday *₹12,000*
- Extra Adult: *₹2,000* per person/night (meals included)
- Kids (5–12 yrs): *₹1,000* per kid/night (meals included)
- Kids under 5: Complimentary / Free (meals included)

# Digital Diaries Screening & Privacy Policies

- **Privacy / No CCTV Policy:** There are STRICTLY NO CCTV cameras inside the private screening rooms. 100% privacy guaranteed.
- **Location:** Wadala, Mumbai.
- **Outside Food:** Outside food is **STRICTLY NOT ALLOWED**. We provide a complimentary snack hamper and have an in-house food menu.
- **Outside Cake:** Bringing your own cake from outside is **STRICTLY NOT ALLOWED**. The Celebration Package includes a 250g cake with custom message. For Movie Time, a 250g cake add-on is available for ₹400.
- **Age Restriction (STRICTLY ENFORCED — NO EXCEPTIONS):**
  - **ALL guests must be 18+ with a valid government ID** (Aadhaar, Driving License, Passport, Voter ID, PAN Card).
  - **Each adult guest must carry their own separate valid ID.** One ID for the whole group is NOT acceptable.
  - IDs will be verified at check-in. Guests without valid ID will NOT be allowed entry.
  - **Under 18 guests are NOT permitted** unless they fall under the Kids/Family Exception below.
- **Kids/Family Exception (ONLY in explicit family context):**
  - Kids are allowed **ONLY when a parent/guardian explicitly states they are bringing their child/kid** using clear family language such as: "my kid", "my child", "baccha", "beti", "beta", "son", "daughter", "family".
  - Kids under 5 years: Complimentary / Free.
  - Kids aged 5 to 18 years: ₹150 per child.
  - The accompanying adult parent/guardian must carry their own valid government ID. No separate ID required for the child.
  - Do NOT proactively mention kids pricing unless the customer specifically asks about bringing children.
- **CRITICAL — Underage Couple / Ambiguous "Under 18" Rule:**
  - If a customer says someone in their group is "under 18", "18 se kam", "minor", or similar **WITHOUT explicitly using family/child language** (kid, child, baccha, beti, beta, son, daughter) → **DO NOT assume it is a family/child situation.**
  - In such cases, **strictly enforce the 18+ rule** and respond: "All guests must be 18+ with valid government ID. We cannot accommodate guests under 18 unless they are children accompanying their parent/family."
  - **NEVER** relax the ID or age requirement for ambiguous situations. When in doubt, enforce 18+.
- **Office Hours:** 10:00 AM to 12:00 AM (midnight). Phone calls will not be answered after 12:00 AM until next day morning.

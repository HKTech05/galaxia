# Digital Diaries Payment & Booking Rules

## Standard Payment & Advance Booking Policy
- **Website Booking Requirement**: All bookings MUST strictly be made online via the official website (https://www.galaxiaresorts.com/digitaldiaries or direct screen links).
- **Accepted Online Payment Modes**: Any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) or Credit/Debit Cards on the website payment gateway.
- **50% Advance & Balance Cash Payment**:
  - **50% of the total booking amount** must be paid online on the website as an advance booking fee to lock the slot.
  - The **remaining 50% balance** must be paid in **CASH at the venue** upon arrival during check-in for the booking.

## Full Cash Payment Options (100% Cash Booking)
If a guest explicitly requests to pay 100% in CASH for a booking, explain the two available options:

1. **Option A (Advance Venue Visit)**:
   - Guests can visit the Digital Diaries venue in person up to a few days prior to their booking date (or early in the morning on the same day for afternoon/evening bookings).
   - They can pay either 50% advance or 100% full payment in cash directly at the venue to lock their slot.
2. **Option B (Website Check & Walk-in)**:
   - Guests can check real-time slot availability on the website at any day/time and walk in directly to make full cash payment on the spot.
   - **Important Disclaimer**: Walk-in cash bookings are **NOT guaranteed**. Anyone else can book the slot online at any time. If the slot gets sold out online before the guest arrives at the venue, the slot will not be available.

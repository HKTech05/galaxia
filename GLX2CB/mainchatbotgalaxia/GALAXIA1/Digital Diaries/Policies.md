# Digital Diaries Screening & Privacy Policies

- **Privacy / No CCTV Policy:** There are STRICTLY NO CCTV cameras inside the private screening rooms. 100% privacy guaranteed.
- **Location:** Wadala, Mumbai.
- **Outside Food:** Outside food is allowed.
- **Age Restriction:** Strictly 18+ for all adult guests. Valid government ID required.
- **Kids Policy:** Kids are allowed when accompanied by an adult. Kids under 5 years are complimentary (free). Kids aged 5 to 18 years are charged ₹150 per child. Do NOT proactively mention kids pricing unless the customer asks about bringing children.

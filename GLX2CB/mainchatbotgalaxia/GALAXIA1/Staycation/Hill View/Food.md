# Hill View Food Options

- **Meals:** Food is NOT included in the base tariff.
- **Restaurant:** There is a restaurant located just 5 steps away from the property (inside Holiday Maiyaan society) that serves both veg and non-veg food.
- **Veg Food in Property:** You can order veg food from the restaurant and have it delivered to the property / eat at your own comfort inside the property.
- **Non-Veg Policy:** Non-veg food must strictly be consumed at the restaurant only — non-veg food is NOT allowed inside the property.
- **Kitchen:** A small kitchen is available with basic utensils, induction cooktop, and fridge for basic self-cooking.

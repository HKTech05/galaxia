- Max Capacity: 8 Persons Total Limit.
- **Max Adult Limit**: **STRICT MAX 6 ADULTS** (Up to 6 Adults + 2 Kids).
- Kids age range: 5 to 12 years (₹400/night). Under 5: Free.
- Extra Adult: ₹600 per night.

*MON to THUR*

# Mount View Pricing & Official Directory

*MOUNT VIEW*

https://www.galaxiaresorts.com/staycation

Apartment includes:
- 1 room with queen size bed
- 1 room with single sofa cum bed
- 1 private bathtub
- 1 huge open to mountains balcony
- 1 Small size kitchen with basic utensils
- 2 washrooms
- 1 Smart TV
- 1 music player
- 1 Induction
- 2 Air conditioner
- 1 Battery backup Invertor
- WiFi / Free parking

The package does not include food though there is a restaurant which provides non-veg and veg food both.

Check-in - 2 PM
Check-out - 10 AM

## Occupancy Limits
- Max Capacity: 8 Persons Total Limit.
- **Max Adult Limit**: **STRICT MAX 6 ADULTS** (Up to 6 Adults + 2 Kids).
- Kids age range: 5 to 12 years (₹500/night). Under 5: Free.
- Extra Adult: ₹800 per night.

*RENT:*
*PRIME DATES:* 5950/- for 2 Person

*MON to THUR:* 3000/- per night for 2 person + 5%
*FRI-SUN AND PUBLIC HOLIDAYS:* 4000/- for 2 person + 5%

Food is excluded.

800/- per person extra after 2
Kids age 5 to 12 yrs: -500/-

30 Dec: 5450/-
31 Dec (New Year): 10000/-
Extra person on Dec 31st: 2500/-

Security deposit Rs 3000/- not included in stay prices, has to be paid during check-in and refunded within 24 hrs of check-out.
Once the booking is done, it's non-refundable and non-transferable in any condition.

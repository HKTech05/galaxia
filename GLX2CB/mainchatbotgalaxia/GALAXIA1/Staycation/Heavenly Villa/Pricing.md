# Heavenly Villa Pricing & Official Directory

*HEAVENLY VILLA*

Villa includes:
- 1 studio room with queen size bed
- 1 single sofa cum bed
- 1 swing near pool
- 1 Small size kitchen with basic utensils
- 1 Smart TV
- 1 music player
- 1 Induction
- 1 Air conditioner
- 1 Private indoor swimming pool
- 1 Battery backup Invertor
- WiFi / Free parking

The package does not include food though there is a restaurant which provides non-veg and veg food both.

Check-in - 2 PM
Check-out - 10 AM

## Occupancy Limits
- Max Capacity: 4 Persons Total Limit.
- **Max Adult Limit**: **STRICT MAX 3 ADULTS** (Up to 3 Adults + 1 Kid).
- Kids age range: 5 to 12 years (₹500/night). Under 5: Free.
- Extra Adult: ₹800 per night.

*RENT:*
*MON to THUR:* 3950/- for 2 person + 5% (Meals not included)
*FRI-SUN AND PUBLIC HOLIDAYS:* 4950/- for 2 person + 5%

800/- per person extra after 2
Kids age 5 to 12 yrs: -500/-

Security deposit Rs 3000/- not included in stay prices, has to be paid during check-in and refunded on check-out.
Once the booking is done, it's non-refundable and non-transferable in any condition.

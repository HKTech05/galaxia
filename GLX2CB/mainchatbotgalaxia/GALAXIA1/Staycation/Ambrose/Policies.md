# Ambrose Villa Policies

All rules, regulations, and policies for Ambrose Villas are the same as Amstel Nest, with the following specific differences noted below.

## Check-in / Check-out
- **Check-in:** 2:00 PM
- **Check-out:** 10:00 AM
- **Late Checkout Fees:**
  - Up to 12 PM or 1 PM: +₹1,500
  - Up to 2 PM or 3 PM: +₹2,500 (includes lunch on checkout day)
  - 4 PM to 7 PM: +₹4,000 (includes lunch on checkout day)
  - After 7 PM: Full next day price added
- **Mon-Thu 21-Hour Flexi Check-in:** Custom check-in/out times allowed at no extra charge on weekdays, provided it is a 21-hour window.

## Pet Policy
- **Pets ARE allowed** at Ambrose Villas.
- Extra charge: ₹600 per pet per night.

## Swimming Pool
- **Private Pool:** Every Ambrose villa has its own private outdoor swimming pool.
- There is NO common/canal swimming pool at Ambrose (unlike Amstel Nest which has a boating canal). Do not mention this unless explicitly asked.

## Food & Dining
- **Meals Included:** Lunch, Dinner, and Breakfast are included in the tariff.
- **Pure Veg Only:** Vegetarian meals ONLY. Non-veg food is strictly prohibited.
- **Jain Food:** Available — select Jain food option while booking on website.
- **Food Menu:** Same as Amstel Nest. Menu link: https://galaxiaresorts.com/menus/ambrose-amstel-menu.jpeg
- **Food Timings:** Breakfast 9:00-10:00 AM, Lunch 1:00-2:45 PM, Dinner 8:30-9:45 PM.
- **Food Menu Changes:** NO menu changes unless group size is 15+ guests.
- **High Tea:** A la carte snacks available.
- **Infant/Child Food:** Chef can prepare special food/milk/khichdi on request.

## Security Deposit
- ₹3,000 refundable deposit required at check-in (Cash or UPI).
- Refunded at checkout (Cash immediately, UPI within 24 hours).
- Not included in booking price.

## Booking & Payment
- Official website booking only.
- 80% advance online, 20% balance at check-in.
- Booking is non-refundable and non-transferable.

## Cancellation Policy
- 21+ days before check-in: 10% deduction
- 11-20 days before check-in: 50% retained
- Within 10 days: Strictly NO refund
- Festivals/Peak dates: Strictly non-refundable

## Max Stay & Other Rules
- Maximum stay duration: 3 consecutive nights.
- Alcohol/drinks allowed (guests must carry their own).
- Hookah smoking is allowed. Call +91 81695 19564 to inquire about provision.
- No decoration provided. Call booking number for special requests.
- Toiletries provided: Towel, soap, shampoo, shower gel. Carry own toothbrush/toothpaste.
- Caretaker: Ranjit (+91 73556 30009).
- Booking phone: +91 81695 19564.

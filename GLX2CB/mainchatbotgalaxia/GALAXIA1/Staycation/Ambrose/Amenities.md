# Ambrose Villa Amenities & Themes

- **Private Swimming Pool:** Every villa features its own private outdoor swimming pool.
- **NO Common/Canal Pool:** Ambrose does NOT have a common swimming pool or boating canal (unlike Amstel Nest).
- **Themes & Villas:**
  - *Take-1:* Bollywood theme villa (Base rate for 2 persons, max 6 adults)
  - *Alta:* Rustic theme villa (Base rate for 2 persons, max 6 adults)
  - *Santorini:* Greek theme villa (Base rate for 2 persons, max 6 adults)
  - *Cypress:* Machan treehouse theme villa (Base rate for 2 persons, max 3 adults)
  - *Bamboosa:* Bali theme 2-bedroom villa (Base rate for 4 persons, max 10 adults)
- **Villa Features (Take-1, Alta, Santorini each):**
  - 1 room with sofa cum bed
  - 1 room with king size bed
  - 1 Smart TV, 2 Air Conditioners, 2 Washrooms
  - 1 Private pool, 1 Garden sitting area
- **General Amenities:** Air conditioning, Smart TV, private garden, open sitting area, power backup.

# Amstel Nest FAQ

- **Q: Are pets allowed?**
  - A: No, pets are strictly not allowed at Amstel Nest.
- **Q: Is non-veg food available?**
  - A: No, Amstel Nest is a strict vegetarian property.
- **Q: Is the pool private?**
  - A: Yes, each cottage has a private indoor swimming pool.
- **Q: Can I book for 10 days?**
  - A: No, maximum stay is 3 nights per booking.
- **Q: What is the address / location / Google Maps link?**
  - A: Amstel Nest is located in Karjat, approximately 30-40 minutes from Karjat railway station by auto-rickshaw or cab.

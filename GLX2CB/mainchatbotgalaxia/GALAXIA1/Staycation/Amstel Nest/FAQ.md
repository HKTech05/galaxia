# Amstel Nest FAQ

- **Q: Are pets allowed?**
  - A: No, pets are strictly not allowed at Amstel Nest. Pet-friendly alternatives: Ambrose Villas, La Paraiso, Mount View, Hill View (₹600/pet/night extra).
- **Q: Is non-veg food available?**
  - A: No, Amstel Nest is a strict vegetarian property.
- **Q: Is the pool private?**
  - A: Yes, each cottage has its own private indoor swimming pool. There is also a common swimming pool (boating canal) on the resort grounds.
- **Q: Can I book for 10 days?**
  - A: No, maximum stay is 3 nights per booking.
- **Q: What is the address / location / Google Maps link?**
  - A: Amstel Nest is located in Karjat, approximately 30-40 minutes from Karjat railway station by auto-rickshaw or cab.
  - Google Maps: https://maps.app.goo.gl/LKBK3GAZZ4G5pR3XA
  - Local auto-rickshaw contact: Mahesh: +91 92847 96472

- **Q: What is the food menu?**
  - A: You can view the full food menu here: https://galaxiaresorts.com/menus/ambrose-amstel-menu.jpeg
  - All meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) are included. Pure veg only.

- **Q: Is Jain food available?**
  - A: Yes, you can select the Jain food option while booking on the website. No need to call in advance. However, if your group has a mix of Jain and regular food preferences, please call +91 99877 34458 to confirm the split.

- **Q: Is day picnic available?**
  - A: Yes, day picnic is available. However, pricing remains the same as a regular 1-night stay. For special day-visit requirements, call +91 99877 34458 during office hours (10 AM to 8 PM).

- **Q: What are the office hours?**
  - A: Office hours are 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Phone calls will only be answered during these hours.

- **Q: Is there parking?**
  - A: Parking is available near the property. However, parking must be done outside the resort premises.

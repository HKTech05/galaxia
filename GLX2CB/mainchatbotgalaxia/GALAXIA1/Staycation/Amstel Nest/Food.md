# Amstel Nest Food & Dining

- **Included:** Lunch, Dinner, and Breakfast are included in the tariff.
- **Dining Policy:** Vegetarian meals ONLY. Non-vegetarian food is strictly PROHIBITED.
- **Jain Food:** Guests can select the Jain food option while booking on the website. No need to call in advance. If the group has a mix of Jain and regular food preferences, call +91 99877 34458 to confirm the split.
- **Direct Clickable Food Menu Link**: https://galaxiaresorts.com/menus/ambrose-amstel-menu.jpeg
- **Driver Accommodation & Meals:** ₹1,000 (or ₹1,500) per driver. Includes mattress, basic accommodation in the reception/common area, and meals.

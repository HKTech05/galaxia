# Amstel Nest Policies

- **Pet Policy:** Pets are strictly NOT allowed at Amstel Nest. Pet-friendly alternatives: Ambrose Villas, La Paraiso, Mount View, Hill View (₹600/pet/night extra).
- **Max Stay Limit:** Maximum stay duration is 3 consecutive nights.
- **Booking Rule:** Bookings can only be confirmed via the official website. Chatbot cannot create or confirm bookings.
- **Security Deposit:** ₹2,000 refundable security deposit required at check-in.
- **Office Hours:** 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Phone calls will only be answered during office hours.
- **Day Picnic:** Day picnic is available. Pricing remains the same as a regular 1-night stay. For special requests, call +91 99877 34458 during office hours.

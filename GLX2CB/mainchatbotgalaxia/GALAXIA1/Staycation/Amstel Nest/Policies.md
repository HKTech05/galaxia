# Amstel Nest Policies

- **Pet Policy:** Pets are strictly NOT allowed at Amstel Nest.
- **Max Stay Limit:** Maximum stay duration is 3 consecutive nights.
- **Booking Rule:** Bookings can only be confirmed via the official website. Chatbot cannot create or confirm bookings.
- **Security Deposit:** ₹2,000 refundable security deposit required at check-in.

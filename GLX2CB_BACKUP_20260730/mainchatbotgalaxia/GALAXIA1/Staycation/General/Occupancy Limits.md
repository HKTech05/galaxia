# Property Occupancy & Max Adult Limits Directory

This document defines the strict maximum adult and total guest capacity thresholds for all Galaxia Staycation properties.

## Property Occupancy Schedule

### 1. Amstel Nest Standard Cottage (14 Units)
- **Max Capacity**: 4 Persons Total Limit.
- **Max Adult Limit**: **STRICT MAX 3 ADULTS**.
- **Allowed Guest Combinations**:
  - 3 Adults + 1 Kid
  - 2 Adults + 2 Kids
  - 1 Adult + 3 Kids
- *Note*: 4 Adults in 1 Standard Cottage is **STRICTLY PROHIBITED**.

### 2. Amstel Nest Family Cottage (1 Unit)
- **Max Capacity**: 8 Persons Total Limit.
- **Max Adult Limit**: **STRICT MAX 6 ADULTS**.
- **Allowed Guest Combinations**:
  - 6 Adults + 2 Kids
  - 5 Adults + 3 Kids
  - 4 Adults + 4 Kids
  - 3 Adults + 5 Kids
  - 2 Adults + 6 Kids
  - 1 Adult + 7 Kids

### 3. Ambrose Theme Villas (Take-1, Alta, Santorini)
- **Max Capacity**: 8 Persons Total Limit.
- **Max Adult Limit**: **STRICT MAX 6 ADULTS**.
- **Combination**: Up to 6 Adults + 2 Kids (Kids age 5–12).

### 4. Ambrose Bamboosa (Large Group Villa)
- **Max Capacity**: 12 Persons Total Limit.
- **Max Adult Limit**: **STRICT MAX 10 ADULTS**.
- **Combination**: Up to 10 Adults + 2 Kids (Kids age 5–12).

### 5. Ambrose Cypress & Heavenly Villa
- **Max Capacity**: 4 Persons Total Limit.
- **Max Adult Limit**: **STRICT MAX 3 ADULTS**.
- **Combination**: Up to 3 Adults + 1 Kid (Kids age 5–12).

### 6. La Paraiso, Mount View & Hill View
- **Max Capacity**: 8 Persons Total Limit.
- **Max Adult Limit**: **STRICT MAX 6 ADULTS**.
- **Combination**: Up to 6 Adults + 2 Kids (Kids age 5–12).

---

## Universal Rules
- **Kids Age Threshold**: Kids age range is strictly **5 to 12 years** across ALL properties.
- **Kids Under 5 Years**: Complimentary / Free stay.
- **Extra Adult Fee**: ₹2,000/night (Amstel Nest, Ambrose), ₹1,200/night (La Paraiso), ₹800/night (Mount View, Heavenly Villa), ₹600/night (Hill View).
